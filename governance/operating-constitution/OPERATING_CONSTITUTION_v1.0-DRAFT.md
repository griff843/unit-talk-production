# PREAMBLE

Unit Talk exists to create the most disciplined, transparent, and structurally
governed betting intelligence platform in the market.

The company rejects hype, opacity, and short-term gimmicks in favor of
measurable performance, operational integrity, and long-term edge development.

This Operating Constitution establishes the financial, governance, compensation,
and control framework under which Unit Talk shall operate.

All participants acknowledge that sustainability, discipline, and structural
integrity take precedence over short-term speculation or opportunistic gain.

# SECTION I — COMPANY STRUCTURE & CONTROL

## ARTICLE 1 — OWNERSHIP STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Equity Allocation

Unit Talk equity is allocated as follows:

- Founder: 80%
- Investor A: 10%
- Investor B: 10%

All investor shares are:

- Non-voting
- Non-operational
- Passive
- Entitled only to declared profit distributions or exit proceeds

Investors retain no authority over:

- Product decisions
- Operational direction
- Hiring or firing
- Capper eligibility
- Model governance
- Capital allocation

Founder retains 100% operational authority.

---

## ARTICLE 2 — DECISION AUTHORITY

### 2.1 Founder Control

Founder retains exclusive authority over:

- Strategic direction
- Product roadmap
- Governance amendments
- Revenue allocation structure
- Compensation frameworks
- Investor communication
- Capital reinvestment policy

No operational veto rights exist for investors.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — PROFIT DISTRIBUTION POLICY

### 3.1 Profit Definition

Net Profit is defined as:

Total revenue  
Minus operating expenses  
Minus capper pool distributions  
Minus contractor payments  
Minus infrastructure costs  
Minus retained growth allocation

Profit is calculated quarterly unless otherwise declared.

---

### 3.2 Distribution Conditions

Profit distributions may only occur if:

- Minimum operating reserve threshold is met.
- Growth capital allocation requirements are satisfied.
- No active architectural freeze is in effect.

Distribution is not guaranteed and is discretionary based on financial
stability.

---

### 3.3 Equity Payout

Declared profits are distributed according to equity ownership:

- Founder: 80%
- Investor A: 10%
- Investor B: 10%

No investor is entitled to fixed payout, salary, or guaranteed return.

# SECTION II — REVENUE STRUCTURE & CAPITAL POLICY

## ARTICLE 4 — REVENUE MODEL

### 4.1 Subscription Tiers

Unit Talk operates on a subscription-based model with the following tiers:

- VIP — $49.99/month
- VIP+ — $99.99/month
- Black Label — $199.99/month

Additional annual billing options may be offered at founder discretion.

Tier entitlements are defined by the Platform Constitution and may evolve over
time.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — REVENUE PRIORITY ORDER

All gross revenue shall be allocated in the following order of priority:

1. Payment processing fees
2. Data provider costs
3. Infrastructure and hosting costs
4. Contractor and operator compensation
5. Capper performance pool allocation
6. Growth allocation reserve
7. Net profit eligibility pool

No profit distribution shall occur before all above categories are satisfied.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — CAPPPER COMPENSATION STRUCTURE

### 6.1 Capper Pool Model

Cappers are compensated via a dynamic performance pool.

Initial allocation:

- 30% of gross subscription revenue

This percentage may increase to:

- 35% when monthly recurring revenue exceeds $50,000
- 40% when monthly recurring revenue exceeds $100,000

Adjustments require founder approval and documentation in the Amendment Log.

---

### 6.2 Pool Distribution Criteria

Pool allocation is weighted by:

- Verified units won
- Closing Line Value (CLV)
- ROI
- Volume integrity
- Adherence to submission rules
- Consistency and availability

No capper is entitled to fixed revenue share unless explicitly contracted.

---

### 6.3 Minimum Performance Standards

Cappers are subject to performance review each quarter.

Failure thresholds include:

- Sustained negative CLV
- Extended negative ROI beyond defined tolerance band
- Failure to meet minimum submission standards
- Behavioral or operational violations

Founder retains authority to remove, suspend, or restructure capper
compensation.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — GROWTH ALLOCATION

### 7.1 Mandatory Growth Reserve

A minimum percentage of gross revenue shall be retained for reinvestment.

Early-stage threshold:

- Minimum 20% of gross revenue retained until $50,000 MRR is achieved.

Post-scale threshold:

- Founder discretion based on strategic priorities.

Growth allocation funds may be used for:

- Infrastructure upgrades
- Marketing expansion
- Personnel scaling
- Tool development
- Data acquisition
- Security and redundancy

---

## ARTICLE 8 — OPERATING RESERVE POLICY

A minimum operating reserve equivalent to:

- 6 months of average fixed operating expenses

Must be maintained before profit distribution eligibility.

Distributions are paused if reserve falls below threshold.

# SECTION III — CAPPER PERFORMANCE GOVERNANCE

## ARTICLE 9 — PERFORMANCE FRAMEWORK

Unit Talk operates under a hybrid performance governance model:

- Objective minimum thresholds define eligibility.
- Founder discretion governs pool weighting and retention decisions.

---

## ARTICLE 10 — OBJECTIVE MINIMUM THRESHOLDS

To remain eligible for pool participation, a capper must:

1. Maintain non-negative Closing Line Value (CLV) over a rolling 90-day period.
2. Maintain ROI within acceptable tolerance band:
   - No worse than -5% over 90 days.
3. Submit a minimum defined volume of plays per month (founder defined per
   sport).
4. Adhere strictly to submission integrity rules.
5. Maintain professional conduct within community.

Failure to meet minimum thresholds may result in:

- Pool weight reduction
- Probation status
- Temporary suspension
- Removal from platform

---

## ARTICLE 11 — FOUNDER DISCRETION AUTHORITY

The founder retains authority to:

- Adjust pool weighting based on qualitative factors.
- Consider variance context (injury waves, market shifts).
- Protect long-term strategic contributors.
- Remove cappers despite meeting thresholds if misaligned with brand integrity.
- Retain cappers temporarily during justified drawdown periods.

Discretion must be documented internally in the Decision Log.

---

## ARTICLE 12 — TRANSPARENCY POLICY

Public capper statistics may include:

- Units
- Win rate
- ROI

Internal metrics may additionally include:

- CLV
- Market timing efficiency
- Edge score
- Volatility profile

Proprietary metrics remain confidential and protected as trade secrets.

# SECTION IV — CONTRACTORS, OPERATORS & COMMISSION STRUCTURE

## ARTICLE 13 — CONTRACTOR STATUS

All non-founder personnel including:

- Moderators
- Operators
- Traders
- Marketing staff
- Designers
- Support staff

Are classified as independent contractors unless otherwise defined.

Contractors:

- Hold no equity.
- Hold no voting rights.
- Hold no profit participation rights.
- Are compensated strictly per agreed compensation terms.

---

## ARTICLE 14 — COMPENSATION STRUCTURES

Contractors may be compensated via:

1. Fixed monthly stipend.
2. Performance-based commission.
3. Hybrid (base + performance).
4. Project-based one-time payments.

All compensation structures must be documented in written agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 15 — OPERATOR / TRADER COMMISSION MODEL

Where traders or operators contribute performance-based insight:

Compensation may be structured as:

- Fixed monthly base (example: $200–$400).
- Performance bonus tied to:
  - Subscriber growth.
  - Engagement metrics.
  - Verified edge contribution.

Traders acting as moderators must not:

- Privately front-run official picks.
- Trade against official selections.
- Disclose proprietary internal metrics.

Violation results in immediate termination eligibility.

---

## ARTICLE 16 — ROLE STACKING POLICY

Personnel may hold multiple roles (e.g., Moderator + Trader + Marketing).

However:

- Compensation stacking must be explicitly defined.
- No automatic entitlement to multiple commissions without defined scope.
- All performance-based compensation must be measurable.

---

## ARTICLE 17 — PERFORMANCE REVIEW

Contractors are reviewed quarterly based on:

- Reliability
- Professional conduct
- Contribution to growth
- Alignment with brand standards

Founder retains unilateral authority to restructure or terminate contractor
agreements.

---

## ARTICLE 18 — CONFIDENTIALITY & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All contractors must sign confidentiality agreement.

All models, systems, data, scoring methods, and analytics:

- Remain sole property of Unit Talk.
- Are protected as proprietary trade secrets.

# SECTION V — PROFIT DISTRIBUTION & FOUNDER COMPENSATION

## ARTICLE 19 — FOUNDER COMPENSATION

The founder may receive compensation in two forms:

1. Monthly operating salary (classified as operating expense).
2. Quarterly profit distribution (based on equity ownership).

Founder salary:

- Is treated as fixed operating expense.
- Must be sustainable relative to revenue.
- May be adjusted at founder discretion based on company performance.

Early-stage guideline:

- Founder salary should not exceed 25% of gross monthly revenue.

---

## ARTICLE 20 — PROFIT DISTRIBUTION SCHEDULE

Profit distribution eligibility is evaluated quarterly.

Distribution conditions:

- All operating expenses satisfied.
- Capper pool obligations satisfied.
- Growth allocation satisfied.
- Operating reserve threshold maintained.

If these conditions are not met, distribution is deferred.

---

## ARTICLE 21 — DISTRIBUTION STRUCTURE

Standard equity distribution:

- Founder: 80%
- Investor A: 10%
- Investor B: 10%

Distributions are calculated from eligible net profit only.

No guaranteed distributions exist.

---

## ARTICLE 22 — REINVESTMENT OVERRIDE AUTHORITY

Founder retains authority to suspend profit distributions for strategic reasons
including:

- Infrastructure upgrades
- Product expansion
- Risk mitigation
- Market downturn response
- Security or compliance needs

Override decisions must be logged in Decision Log.

---

## ARTICLE 23 — EMERGENCY FREEZE CLAUSE

In the event of:

- Significant revenue decline
- Operational disruption
- Data provider failure
- Regulatory change
- Legal exposure

All profit distributions may be temporarily frozen until stability is restored.

# SECTION VI — EXIT, SALE & DISSOLUTION

## ARTICLE 24 — SALE OF COMPANY

The founder retains full authority to initiate sale discussions, strategic
partnerships, or acquisition negotiations.

Because the founder holds majority ownership (80%), no minority investor
approval is required to pursue a sale.

Minority investors retain economic rights proportional to equity ownership upon
sale.

---

## ARTICLE 25 — EQUITY TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS

Minority investors may not:

- Transfer equity
- Sell equity
- Assign ownership

Without written founder approval.

Founder retains right of first refusal on any proposed equity transfer.

---

## ARTICLE 26 — DISTRIBUTION UPON EXIT

In the event of:

- Acquisition
- Asset sale
- Full company sale
- Dissolution

Net proceeds shall be distributed according to equity ownership:

- Founder: 80%
- Investor A: 10%
- Investor B: 10%

Unless otherwise negotiated in formal written agreement.

---

## ARTICLE 27 — DISSOLUTION

In the event of company shutdown:

Remaining assets after liabilities shall be distributed proportionally by
equity.

Founder retains authority to determine timing and process of orderly wind-down.

# SECTION VII — NON-COMPETE & NON-SOLICITATION

## ARTICLE 28 — NON-COMPETE (CAPPERS & CONTRACTORS)

All cappers and contractors agree that during their active engagement and for a
period of 12 months following termination:

They shall not:

- Operate or participate in a competing paid sports betting subscription
  service.
- Launch a Discord, Telegram, or similar paid pick service.
- Provide paid betting intelligence services to a competing entity.
- Replicate Unit Talk proprietary models, metrics, or system structures.

This clause applies only to paid or monetized competitive activity.

Public personal betting content (non-monetized) is not restricted unless it
directly competes with Unit Talk offerings.

---

## ARTICLE 29 — NON-SOLICITATION

Cappers and contractors shall not:

- Directly solicit Unit Talk subscribers for alternative services.
- Encourage members to migrate to external paid platforms.
- Use internal performance data for external marketing.
- Privately monetize relationships formed within Unit Talk ecosystem.

Violation may result in:

- Immediate termination.
- Forfeiture of unpaid compensation.
- Legal enforcement if necessary.

---

## ARTICLE 30 — PROPRIETARY INFORMATION

All performance analytics, scoring models, automation logic, and internal
metrics are classified as confidential trade secrets.

Unauthorized disclosure constitutes breach of contract.

---

## ARTICLE 31 — MINIMUM ENGAGEMENT TERM (CAPPERS)

All cappers agree to a minimum engagement term of 90 days upon onboarding.

During this period:

- Capper participation in pool remains active unless terminated for cause.
- Early voluntary withdrawal requires 30-day written notice.
- Founder retains authority to terminate immediately for performance failure or
  misconduct.

The 90-day term ensures:

- Performance evaluation stability.
- Subscriber continuity.
- Protection against short-term opportunistic participation.

# SECTION VIII — AMENDMENT AUTHORITY

## ARTICLE 32 — AMENDMENT RIGHTS

The Founder retains exclusive authority to amend, modify, expand, or revise this
Operating Constitution at any time.

Minority investors:

- Do not retain amendment voting rights.
- Do not retain veto authority.
- Do not retain operational governance authority.

All amendments must be:

- Documented in the Amendment Log.
- Dated and versioned.
- Stored within the governance repository.

This authority ensures agility, strategic flexibility, and protection of
long-term platform integrity.

# SECTION IX — SUCCESSION & INCAPACITY

## ARTICLE 33 — FOUNDER INCAPACITY OR DEATH

In the event of the Founder’s permanent incapacity or death:

1. Operational control shall transfer to a temporary appointed executor as
   defined in Founder’s legal estate documents.
2. Equity ownership shall transfer according to legal estate planning documents.
3. Minority investors retain economic rights only and do not gain operational
   control.
4. The company may appoint an interim operator to preserve continuity.

This clause ensures stability and prevents automatic operational control
transfer to minority equity holders.

# SECTION X — FUTURE CAPITALIZATION & EQUITY ISSUANCE

## ARTICLE 34 — AUTHORITY TO ISSUE ADDITIONAL EQUITY

The Founder retains authority to:

- Issue new equity.
- Raise external capital.
- Restructure ownership percentages.
- Create new share classes.

Minority investors acknowledge that:

- Future capital raises may dilute ownership.
- No anti-dilution protection is guaranteed unless formally negotiated.
- No pre-emptive rights are granted by default.

All capitalization changes must be documented in the Amendment Log.

# SECTION XI — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

## ARTICLE 35 — GOVERNING LAW

This Operating Constitution shall be governed by and construed in accordance
with the laws of the Founder’s state of residence.

---

## ARTICLE 36 — BINDING ARBITRATION

Any dispute arising out of or relating to:

- Equity ownership
- Profit distribution
- Capper compensation
- Contractor agreements
- Governance interpretation

Shall be resolved through binding arbitration conducted in the Founder’s state
of residence.

Parties waive:

- Right to jury trial.
- Right to public court litigation, except where legally prohibited.

Each party shall bear its own legal fees unless otherwise determined by the
arbitrator.

---

## ARTICLE 37 — CONFIDENTIALITY OF PROCEEDINGS

All arbitration proceedings shall remain confidential.

Disclosure of proprietary systems, models, or financial data during dispute
resolution shall remain protected.
